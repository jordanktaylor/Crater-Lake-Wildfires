Wildfires of Crater Lake in Oregon (1992-2020)

by Jordan Taylor

I visited Oregon on vacation in August of 2024 and one of the highlights of the trip was Crater Lake. My wife and I were there during a lot of wildfires, and many roads around the lake were closed off for safety. Being as close as we were to the wildfires (less than 2 miles) and hiking past several fire watch towers along trails, I got fairly interested in the firewatch culture of the area.

Static Map

This map shows the locations of wildfires within a 5-mile radius from Crater Lake in Oregon. Data labels appear when zoomed in, displaying information about the Wildfire Class of the fires and the data that they were discovered Below is a static map as a PNG that displays all the different wildfire locations during this time period, but displayed as different sizes according to the legend. These different denominations denote the "Class" of the wildfires, based on the acreage of the areas they affected.

Interactive Map

The below map was created for interactivity, allowing users to explore the wildfire data in more detail. Labels for the wildfire class and the discovery date of the wildfire will appear once zoomed in further.
